# Claude QC Handoff — Candidate #2

## Overall Decision
**REVISE — Candidate #2 is the current best base, but not ready to lock.**

## Why #2 is the current base
- strongest face-to-body balance among the compared outputs
- clear frontal readability
- good overall silhouette and Instagram attention value
- stable hair / anatomy / subject consistency

## Priority Failures

### 1. Excessive AI-Glamour Direction — HIGH
The whole image is over-resolved toward polished AI glamour rather than believable photographic realism.

Observed:
- overly optimized hourglass presentation
- very clean cosmetic rendering
- body emphasis stronger than natural clothing/body interaction
- overall “designed virtual beauty” impression

**Classification:** repeated/systematic tendency

### 2. Outfit Reference Fidelity / Reference Mixing — HIGH
The clothing does not read as the Outfit Reference being faithfully transferred.

Observed:
- bodysuit construction looks reinterpreted
- shorts placement / shape feels optimized around the body rather than faithfully preserved
- clothing appears influenced by body-shape information
- result feels like BODY reference + OUTFIT reference + model styling prior were blended together

Core diagnosis:
**the system is reproducing the outfit concept, not reliably preserving the specific garment design.**

### 3. Chest Physics — HIGH
Chest shape is still too mechanically clean and idealized.

Observed:
- excessive symmetry
- very orderly central cleavage
- volume sits too “perfectly”
- insufficient natural gravity / lateral distribution
- torso-to-chest transition feels designed rather than physically spontaneous

The issue is not simply size.  
The issue is **overly orderly physical behavior**.

### 4. Skin / Face Rendering — HIGH
Skin remains too homogeneous and AI-beauty-like.

Observed:
- weak local tonal variation
- similar smoothness across face, neck, chest, and arms
- overly clean facial feature edges
- slightly doll-like / cosmetically normalized facial rendering

Identity is reasonably stable, but rendering makes the character feel more generic and synthetic.

## Preserve
- current face/body scale relationship
- frontal composition
- strong silhouette readability
- identity consistency
- hair continuity
- anatomical stability
- strong visual attention value

## Direction Priority for Next Iteration
1. Reduce overall AI-glamour reinterpretation.
2. Improve exact Outfit Reference design fidelity and reduce body/outfit blending.
3. Make chest behavior physically less perfect and less symmetrical.
4. Improve natural skin/face variation without sacrificing identity.
5. Preserve Candidate #2's current proportion balance and structural stability.

## Acceptance Standard for Next Test
The next version should keep Candidate #2's strong proportion/readability while showing:
- less synthetic glamour
- more faithful garment construction
- more believable chest physics
- more photographic skin and face rendering

## Final Status
**CURRENT BEST BASE / REVISE_MINIMALLY / DO NOT LOCK YET**
