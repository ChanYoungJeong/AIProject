BODY_MASTER_USAGE_RULES_v2

1. PRIMARY AUTHORITY
- 02_BODY_MASTER.jpg is the canonical body-proportion authority.
- Preserve its shoulder/torso ratio, torso length, waist, pelvis width, thigh volume and leg proportions.

2. CORE VIEW SUPPORT
- 3Q / SIDE / BACK show the same body from additional viewpoints.
- Use them to resolve depth ambiguity, not to average into a different body.

3. POSE SUPPORT
- Seated, squat, supine, prone, walk and quadruped images show expected pose deformation.
- Compression, gravity, weight shift and perspective can change visible widths and projections.
- Do not convert those visible pose effects into permanent body changes.

4. PHYSICS PRINCIPLE
- Sitting/squatting: thighs and soft tissue can spread/compress.
- Supine: anterior projection reduces and soft tissue redistributes laterally.
- Prone: front torso contact changes chest/abdomen distribution.
- Quadruped: pelvis/spine angle changes apparent projection; do not exaggerate lumbar arch or glute size.
- Walking/weight shift: asymmetry is expected; do not redefine limb lengths.

5. PRODUCTION RULE
- Current NAT_v1.4.3 remains a one-BODY-MASTER production lane unless a newer canonical workflow explicitly enables body support selection.
- Do not feed every support asset simultaneously by default.

6. IDENTITY RULE
- FACE_ID_MASTER remains highest facial authority.
- These body images must not replace or redefine facial identity.
