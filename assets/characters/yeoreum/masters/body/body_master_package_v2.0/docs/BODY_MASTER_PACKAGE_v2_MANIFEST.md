## POSE / DEFORMATION SUPPORT

### `BODY_WALK_WEIGHT_SHIFT_v2.png`
Purpose: natural unilateral weight shift and asymmetry.
Do not use as absolute pelvis/leg width authority.

### `BODY_CHAIR_SEATED_v2.png`
Purpose: seated compression and thigh/pelvis spread under body weight.
Expected physics: thighs appear wider under compression; torso appears shorter.

### `BODY_SQUAT_v2.png`
Purpose: deep squat compression.
Expected physics: thigh widening, hip flexion, shortened torso appearance, knee/ankle flexion.
Use as deformation evidence only.

### `BODY_SUPINE_OVERHEAD_v2.png`
Purpose: body lying on back, orthogonal top-down view.
Expected physics: gravity distributes torso/bust laterally and reduces standing projection.

### `BODY_PRONE_ELBOWS_v2.png`
Purpose: prone body compression with elbows supporting upper body.
Expected physics: front torso contact/pressure and altered chest/abdomen distribution.

### `BODY_QUADRUPED_NEUTRAL_2PANEL_v2.png`
Purpose: neutral quadruped evidence from two useful views.
Expected physics: mild natural spine/pelvis angle only.
Restriction: do not interpret pose-induced projection as a larger canonical glute or smaller waist.

## Package rule

Support images answer: **"How does the same canonical body look from this angle or under this pose?"**
They do not answer: **"What are the canonical dimensions?"**
Canonical dimensions always come from `02_BODY_MASTER.jpg`.
